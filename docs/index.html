Sputnik DAO · NEAR mainnet · USDC

If your group votes to spend money you didn't agree to, take your share out before it pays.

Dissent Finance is a vault that sits next to a group's unchanged Sputnik DAO. A spend the DAO approves waits for a fixed window. Anyone who did not vote for it can leave with their exact share first, and if the vault can no longer pay, the spend is cancelled. Each member's agent watches for them, with a key that can call only exit and cancel_spend.

In 16 Sputnik DAOs, 473 approved transfers went through over at least one Reject vote (427 of them routine payouts in one DAO), and none of those members had a way out. Here the DAO stays exactly as it is, and its members have voted its factory auto-update off.

    How it works
    
    
    The DAO keeps deciding. The vault only adds a way out.
    
    
    
      
    Join
    Members put USDC and a little NEAR into the vault and get shares. The DAO's policy and code are not touched.
    
      
    Set your own exit rules
    Each member says when their agent should leave: one spend over a share of the fund, a receiver never paid before, or a blocked receiver.
    
      
    DAO approves, vault waits
    An approved proposal calls queue_spend. The vault reads the proposal back from the DAO and holds it for the window.
    
      
    Agents act
    Every agent writes a note, hashes it, and exits with the hash if a rule is broken. If the vault can no longer pay, an agent cancels the spend.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Share market
    
    
    Leaving is not the only way out. Members' agents can buy a leaver's shares.
    
    
    A member who wants out without pulling money from the fund offers shares at a reserve price. The other members' market agents bid from USDC they keep in the vault, each by its own member's limits. The highest bid when the window ends buys the shares; the seller gets the USDC and the fund does not move. No shares change hands around a spend: no offers while one is open, and an offer during which one was queued ends unsold. A market agent's key can only bid and settle; offering shares takes the member's own key.
    
    
    
      
    Fund your bids
    A member sends USDC to the vault with bid_funds. It stays theirs and comes back with their exit.
    
      
    Offer shares
    With their own key, a member calls offer_shares: a timed auction with a reserve. Not while a spend is open; one offer per member, eight at most.
    
      
    Agents bid
    Each bid must meet the reserve or beat the best bid by 1%. An agent bids the least that leads, up to its member's price limit and spend cap.
    
      
    Settle
    After the window anyone calls settle_offer: shares to the buyer, USDC to the seller, a share_sale in the vault's record.

    The track asks forHow Dissent Finance meets itProof
    
      
        
    Build on the existing Sputnik DAO, don't write a new multisigThe DAO is a stock one from the public sputnik-dao.near factory. The vault is a separate contract; it only accepts a spend after reading the DAO's own record of the approved proposal.The DAO on mainnet
    
        
    TrezuTrezu lists the DAO like any other treasury. Its requests are the DAO's own proposals: the three spends, each a queue_spend call on a vault with 2 approving votes (2 needed), and the members' vote to turn off the factory's auto-update.Open it in Trezu
    
        
    Shared budgets, collective ownershipMembers own the fund by shares, and nobody can be made to fund a spend they did not approve: they can leave with their exact share while it waits. A member who approved can leave too, but their part of that spend stays behind for it. A member who wants out without taking money from the fund can sell shares to the others instead.Members now, the approver who left, the share sale
    
        
    Programmable rulesEach member's exit rules are stored on chain in the vault: the largest share of the fund one spend may take, whether the receiver must have been paid before (a trust tier the receiver earns by being paid), and blocked receivers. The agent applies them the same way every time, so anyone can replay a decision from its note.Try it with your own rules
    
        
    Named accountsEvery member, agent key and exit is a named account (alice.dissentfinance.near…). The agents' keys are function-call keys on each member's own account.The notes and their hashes
    
        
    Use it with a DAO you already runThree things to set up and no migration: deploy the vault next to the DAO, members join, and spends become ordinary queue_spend proposals. A 0.1% fee on each paid spend is the only revenue.docs/ADOPT.md

    What this is not
    
    
    Plain limits, stated up front
    
    
    
      
    Not a veto
    Dissenters can't stop a spend. They can leave before it pays. If enough leave that the vault can't pay, the spend is cancelled.
    
      
    Not an AI that decides for you
    The agents apply rules each member wrote. The notes show every check and number they used.
    
      
    Not audited
    Small real amounts on mainnet. The contract has not had an external audit.
    
      
    Approvers pay their part
    A member who approved a spend can still leave, but their part of it stays behind for it, so approving and running saves nothing. If the spend is cancelled, they get that part back.
