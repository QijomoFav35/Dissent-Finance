      How it works, in five steps.

      Think of a class jar of pocket money that everyone puts into, and that only pays out when the class votes. Dissent Finance adds one rule to that jar: before a payment goes out, anyone who didn’t vote for it can take their share back.

      New to this? Five words you’ll see

      DAO
      A group that votes on how to spend shared money, with the votes kept on a blockchain. Sputnik DAO is the one most NEAR groups use.
      Vault
      The jar. A program on NEAR that holds the money and follows the steps above.
      Agent
      A small program that watches the vault for you. Its key can do only two things: take your share out, or cancel a payment the vault can’t cover.
      USDC
      A digital dollar. 1 USDC is meant to be worth 1 US dollar.
      Mainnet
      The real NEAR network, with real money. Everything on this page happened there.

        Get started.

        There are three ways in, depending on who you are. The vault only lets in members of the DAO it sits next to, so joining starts with a DAO.

        Look around

        For anyone, no wallet needed

        1. Watch the replay above.
        2. See our DAO and its votes in Trezu, the NEAR treasury app.
        3. Open the vault on NearBlocks and check any number on this page.

        Join a group that uses it

        For a member of a Sputnik DAO with a vault

        1. Be in one of the DAO’s roles. If you aren’t, the DAO votes you in.
        2. Deposit USDC and a little NEAR into the vault. You get shares at the fund’s current price.
        3. Set your three rules: the most one payment may take, “only people paid before”, and a blocked list.
        4. Give your agent a key that can only withdraw or cancel, and start it.

        Today each step is a command; there’s no app yet. The exact commands.

        Add it to your DAO

        For a DAO’s council

        1. Create a vault account next to your DAO (about 3.4 NEAR for its storage) and deploy the published code.
        2. Choose how long payments wait. We suggest a day.
        3. Members deposit. From then on, spending from the vault is an ordinary DAO proposal.
        4. Lock it: turn off the DAO’s automatic code updates and delete the vault’s full-access key.

        Your DAO keeps its members and votes. After setup, the costs are gas for the agents and a 0.1% fee on each payment that goes out. The full guide.

        Want to try it with your group? Start from the code on GitHub. Sputnik DAOs are created through the sputnik-dao.near factory.

        What your agent can and can’t do.

        • It can take your share out.Its key works on one vault and two actions: withdraw your share, or cancel a payment the vault can no longer cover.
        • It can’t send money anywhere else.No transfers, no deposits, no other contracts. The network itself refuses anything else, and those refusals are on record.
        • It only acts on payments the DAO approved.The vault reads the DAO’s own record and checks the payment matches it exactly before holding it.
        • It says why.Every withdrawal carries the hash of the agent’s note. All 3 withdrawals on mainnet match their notes.

        Frequently asked questions

        Is the vault locked yet?

        v2 still has 1 full-access (admin) key, so whoever holds it can replace the vault’s code. v1 has none. This line is read from mainnet each time the page loads.

        Can I stop a payment I don’t like?

        No, it isn’t a veto. You can only withdraw your share before it’s paid. If enough members withdraw, the vault can’t cover the payment and it’s cancelled.

        Can my agent run off with my money?

        No. Its key works on one vault and two actions: withdraw, which sends your share to you, or cancel a payment the vault can’t cover. NEAR itself refuses anything else signed with that key.

        What if I voted yes, then change my mind?

        You can still withdraw, but your part of that payment stays behind and pays it. Changing your mind late saves you nothing and costs the others nothing.

        Do I need a wallet to try it?

        No. The replay, the rules panel and every number on this page work without one. Joining a vault does need a NEAR account.

        What does it cost?

        Members never pay to withdraw. A group pays about 3.4 NEAR once to set up a vault, gas for its agents, and 0.1% of each payment that goes out.

        Is it audited?

        No. It has 82 contract tests and four independent reviews, but no professional audit. Amounts on mainnet are small, and the team runs all five member accounts.

        Does my DAO have to change?

        No. Your Sputnik DAO keeps its members, rules and votes. The vault sits next to it. How a DAO adds it.
